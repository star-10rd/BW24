A sequence of non-negative integers $a_1,\ldots,a_n$ is called self-descriptive if

$$
a_k=\bigl|\{i\mid a_i\ge k\}\bigr|
$$

for each $k\in\{1,\ldots,n\}$, i.e. $a_k$ equals the number of elements of the sequence that are greater than or equal to $k$, for each $k\in\{1,\ldots,n\}$. For each positive integer $n$, determine the number of self-descriptive sequences of length $n$.
