The $2m$ numbers

$$
1\cdot2,\ 2\cdot3,\ 3\cdot4,\ \ldots,\ 2m(2m+1)
$$

are written on a blackboard, where $m\ge2$ is an integer. A move consists of choosing three numbers $a,b,c$, erasing them from the board and writing the single number

$$
\frac{abc}{ab+bc+ca}.
$$

After $m-1$ such moves, only two numbers will remain on the blackboard. Supposing one of these is $\frac43$, show that the other is larger than 4.
