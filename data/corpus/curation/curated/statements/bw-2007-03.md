Suppose that $F,G,H$ are polynomials of degree at most $2n+1$ with real coefficients such that:

1. For all real $x$ we have $F(x)\le G(x)\le H(x)$.
2. There exist distinct real numbers $x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_n$ such that $F(x_i)=H(x_i)$ for $i=1,2,\ldots,n$.
3. There exists a real number $x_0$ different from $x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_n$ such that $F(x_0)+H(x_0)=2G(x_0)$.

Prove that $F(x)+H(x)=2G(x)$ for all real numbers $x$.
