Let $P$ be a point on a segment $AB$. Draw the semicircle with diameter $AB$, and inside it the two semicircles with diameters $AP$ and $PB$, all on the same side of $AB$. Let $PQ$ be the perpendicular to $AB$ through $P$, meeting the large semicircle at $Q$. A circle $C$ lies inside the large semicircle on the side of $PQ$ containing $B$, and is tangent to both smaller semicircles and to the line $PQ$. The area inside the large semicircle but outside the two smaller semicircles and outside $C$ is $39\pi$, and the area of $C$ is $9\pi$. Find the length of $AB$.
