Rewrite the two factors on the left-hand side:

$$
\begin{aligned}
2 S+n & =\left(a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{n}\right)+\left(a_{2}+a_{3}+\cdots a_{1}\right)+1+\cdots+1 \\
2 S+a_{1} a_{2}+a_{2} a_{3}+\cdots+a_{n} a_{1} & =\left(a_{2}+a_{3}+\cdots+a_{1}\right)+\left(a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots a_{n}\right)+a_{1} a_{2}+a_{2} a_{3}+\cdots+a_{n} a_{1}
\end{aligned}
$$

Applying the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality to the $3 n$-vectors

$$
\left(\sqrt{a_{1}}, \ldots, \sqrt{a_{n}}, \sqrt{a_{2}}, \ldots, \sqrt{a_{1}}, 1, \ldots, 1\right) \quad \text { and } \quad\left(\sqrt{a_{2}}, \ldots, \sqrt{a_{1}}, \sqrt{a_{1}}, \ldots \sqrt{a_{n}}, \sqrt{a_{1} a_{2}}, \ldots, \sqrt{a_{n} a_{1}}\right)
$$

we obtain

$$
(2 S+n)\left(2 S+a_{1} a_{2}+a_{2} a_{3}+\cdots a_{n} a_{1}\right) \geq\left(3 \sum_{i=1}^{n} \sqrt{a_{i} a_{i+1}}\right)^{2}
$$

with $a_{n+1}=a_{1}$.
