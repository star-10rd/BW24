Solution:
Denoting the left hand sides of the given equations as $A$, $B$ and $C$, the following equalities can easily be seen to hold:
$$
\begin{aligned}
-A+B+C & =(-a+b+c)^{3} \\
A-B+C & =(a-b+c)^{3} \\
A+B-C & =(a+b-c)^{3} .
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence, the system of equations given in the problem is equivalent to the following one:
$$
\left\{\begin{array}{c}
(-a+b+c)^{3}=1 \\
(a-b+c)^{3}=1 \\
(a+b-c)^{3}=1
\end{array}\right.
$$
which gives
$$
\left\{\begin{array}{c}
-a+b+c=1 \\
a-b+c=1 \\
a+b-c=1
\end{array} .\right.
$$
The unique solution of this system is $(a, b, c)=(1,1,1)$.
