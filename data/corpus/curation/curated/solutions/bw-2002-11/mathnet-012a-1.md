Solution:
We can assume that $|AD| > |AB|$ and $|AD| > |AC|$. By the cosine law we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
|BD|^2 & = |AD|^2 + |AB|^2 - 2|AD||AB| \cos \angle BAD \\
& < |AD|^2 + |AB|^2 - 2|AB|^2 \cos \angle BAD \\
& = |AD|^2 + |AB|^2 (1 - 2 \cos \angle BAD) \\
& \leqslant |AD|^2
\end{aligned}
$$
since $1 \leqslant 2 \cos (\angle BAD)$. Hence $|BD| < |AD|$. Similarly we get $|CD| < |AD|$. Hence $A$ and $D$ should not be connected which is a contradiction.
