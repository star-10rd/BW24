Applying the operator $\frac{d}{dx}$ two times and four times we obtain $-a^2 \cos(ax) - b^2 \cos(bx) = -2c^2 \cos(cx)$ and $a^4 \cos(ax) + b^4 \cos(bx) = 2c^4 \cos(cx)$, respectively. Plugging in $x = 0$ we obtain $a^2 + b^2 = 2c^2$ and $a^4 + b^4 = 2c^4$. Therefore
$$
2(a^4 + b^4) = 4c^4 = (2c^2)^2 = (a^2 + b^2)^2 = a^4 + 2a^2b^2 + b^4,
$$
which yields $0 = \text{LHS} - \text{RHS} = a^4 + b^4 - 2a^2b^2 = (a^2 - b^2)^2$. Therefore $a^2 = b^2$. From $2c^2 = a^2 + b^2 = 2a^2$ we obtain $c^2 = a^2$. Therefore $|a| = |b| = |c|$ and we check directly that all such triples work. $\square$
