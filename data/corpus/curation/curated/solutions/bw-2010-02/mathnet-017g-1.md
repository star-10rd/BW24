The geometric-arithmetic inequality gives
$$
\cos x \sin x \le \frac{\cos^2 x + \sin^2 x}{2} = \frac{1}{2}.
$$
It follows that
$$
1 = (\cos^2 x + \sin^2 x)^2 = \cos^4 x + \sin^4 x + 2 \cos^2 x \sin^2 x \le \cos^4 x + \sin^4 x + \frac{1}{2}
$$
so
$$
\cos^4 x + \sin^4 x \ge \frac{1}{2} \ge \cos x \sin x.
$$
The required inequality follows.
