Solution:

The initial segment of the sequence could be $1; 2; 3; 1; 1; -2; 0$. Clearly it is enough to consider only initial segments. For each sequence the first 6 elements are $a_{1}; a_{2}; a_{3}; a_{2} - a_{1}; a_{3} - a_{2}; a_{2} - a_{1} - a_{3}$. As we see, $a_{1} + a_{5} + a_{6} = a_{1} + (a_{3} - a_{2}) + (a_{2} - a_{1} - a_{3}) = 0$. So all the elements $a_{1}, a_{5}, a_{6}$ can not be positive simultaneously.
