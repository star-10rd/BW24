$$
\begin{aligned}
(1 - a_1^n)(1 - a_2^n) \cdots (1 - a_n^n) &\le \left( \frac{(1 - a_1^n) + (1 - a_2^n) + \cdots + (1 - a_n^n)}{n} \right)^n \\
&= \left( 1 - \frac{a_1^n + \cdots + a_n^n}{n} \right)^n.
\end{aligned}
$$
By applying AM-GM again we obtain
$$
a_1 a_2 \cdots a_n \le \frac{a_1^n + \cdots + a_n^n}{n} \Rightarrow \left(1 - \frac{a_1^n + \cdots + a_n^n}{n}\right)^n \le (1 - a_1 a_2 \cdots a_n)^n,
$$
and hence the desired inequality. □
