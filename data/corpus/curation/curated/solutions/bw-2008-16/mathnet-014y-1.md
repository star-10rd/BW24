Solution:
If the lines $BD$ and $XY$ are parallel the statement is trivial. Let $M$ be the intersection point of $BD$ and $XY$.
By Intercept Theorem $\dfrac{MB}{MD} = \dfrac{MC}{MY}$ and $\dfrac{MB}{MD} = \dfrac{MX}{MC}$, hence $MC^2 = MX \cdot MY$. By the circle property $MC^2 = MP \cdot MQ$ (line $MC$ is tangent and line $MP$ is secant to the circle). Therefore we have $MX \cdot MY = MP \cdot MQ$ and the quadrilateral $PQYX$ is inscribed.
