**Solution.** In figure 15, we have
$$
\angle CNK = \angle ANK = \angle AOK = 2\angle ABK = 2\angle NBK.
$$
Hence triangle $BNK$ is isosceles, so $NK = NB = NC$, and therefore $\angle BKC$ is right.
If we let $KC$ intersect $\omega$ at $K'$, we see that $K', O$ and $B$ are collinear, as $\angle BKK'$ is right.
Let us note that $C$ lies on $OX$. Indeed,
$$
\mathrm{Pow}(C, AONKX) = AC \cdot CN = \frac{1}{2} AC \cdot CB = CM \cdot CB = \mathrm{Pow}(C, BOMLX).
$$
Now,
$$
\angle LXC = \angle LXO = \angle LBO = \angle LBK' = \angle LKK' = \angle LKC
$$
Showing that $XLCK$ is cyclic, as required.
