By the means inequality we have $a^3 + 2 = a^3 + 1 + 1 \ge 3\sqrt[3]{a^3 \cdot 1 \cdot 1} = 3a$. Therefore it is sufficient to prove the inequality
$$
\frac{a}{3a+6} + \frac{b}{3b+6} + \frac{c}{3c+6} + \frac{d}{3d+6} \le \frac{4}{9}.
$$
We can write the last inequality in the form
$$
\frac{1}{a+2} + \frac{1}{b+2} + \frac{1}{c+2} + \frac{1}{d+2} \ge \frac{4}{3}.
$$
Now it follows by the harmonic and arithmetic means inequality:
$$
\frac{1}{4} \left( \frac{1}{a+2} + \frac{1}{b+2} + \frac{1}{c+2} + \frac{1}{d+2} \right) \ge \frac{4}{(a+2)(b+2)(c+2)(d+2)} = \frac{4}{4+2+2+2+2} = \frac{1}{3}.
$$
