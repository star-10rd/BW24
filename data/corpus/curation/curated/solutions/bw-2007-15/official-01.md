Let $B_{1}=D$, and similarly let the incircle touch the sides $A B$ and $B C$ at the points $C_{1}$ and $A_{1}$, respectively. Let the second circle touch the ray $B C$ at the point $M$. Let $x=B_{1} C, y=A B_{1}$. Obviously, $A_{1} M=C_{1} A=$ $A B_{1}=y$ and $A_{1} C=B_{1} C=x$. Hence

$$
C M=A_{1} M-A_{1} C=y-x .
$$

On the other hand, $C M$ is a tangent and $C A$ is a secant to the second circle. Therefore, by powers with respect to this circle,

$$
C M^{2}=C B_{1} \cdot C A=x(x+y)
$$

So we have the equation

$$
(y-x)^{2}=x(x+y) \text {. }
$$

From this equation, we get $y / x=3$.
