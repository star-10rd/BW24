Solution:

Suppose there are such integers. Let us consider the situation modulo $4$. Then each square is $0$ or $1$. But $2006 \equiv 2 \pmod{4}$. So the product of each two supposed numbers must be $2 \pmod{4}$ or $3 \pmod{4}$. From this it follows that there are at least three odd numbers (because the product of two even numbers is $0 \pmod{4}$). Two of these odd numbers are congruent modulo $4$, so their product is $1 \pmod{4}$, which is a contradiction.
