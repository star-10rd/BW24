Solution:
From the GM-HM inequality we have
$$
\frac{1}{AX} + \frac{1}{XY} \geq \frac{2}{\sqrt{AX \cdot XY}}
$$
As $BC$ and $AY$ are chords intersecting at $X$ we have $AX \cdot XY = BX \cdot XC$. Therefore (1) transforms into
$$
\frac{1}{AX} + \frac{1}{XY} \geq \frac{2}{\sqrt{BX \cdot XC}}
$$
We also have
$$
\sqrt{BX \cdot XC} \leq \frac{BX + XC}{2} = \frac{BC}{2}
$$
so from (2) the result follows.
