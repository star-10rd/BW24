Let $E$ be the intersection point of the diagonals $AC$ and $BD$. Let $EF$ be the bisector of $\angle DEC$, with $F$ on $DC$.

![](attached_image_1.png)

Then the triangles $BEC$ and $CFE$ are congruent ($EFCB$ is a kite). So $EF = EB = EA$, and $ADFE$ is a kite. It follows that $\angle DAC = 100^\circ$.
