From $BC = BF$ follows $\triangle BFC = \triangle BCF$. From $\triangle BFC + \triangle BCF = \triangle ABC$ then follows $\triangle BFC = \triangle ABE$. Hence $FC \parallel BE$. Analogously $GB \parallel CD$. Therefore
$$
\frac{AF}{AG} = \frac{AF \ AC \ AB}{AC \ AB \ AG} = \frac{AB \ AC \ AD}{AE \ AB \ AC} = \frac{AD}{AE},
$$
whence the assertion follows.
