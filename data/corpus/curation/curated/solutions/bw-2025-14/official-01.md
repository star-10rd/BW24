## Solution Let BH intersect ω again in P′ . Then ∠CBP′ = 90◦ − ∠ACB = ∠P CB, meaning
that P BCP′ is an isosceles trapezoid. By symmetry, triangles BP R and CP′ R are equal, and
also triangles CRH and CRP′ are symmetrical with respect to AC. Hence there exist rotation
mapping △BRP into △CRH, hence triangles △P RH and △CBR are similar, meaning that
∠HP R = ∠ACB, finishing the proof.
