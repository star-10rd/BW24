## Solution

Let $BH$ meet $\omega$ again at $P'$. Then

$$
\angle CBP'=90^\circ-\angle ACB=\angle PCB,
$$

so $PBCP'$ is an isosceles trapezoid.

By symmetry, triangles $BPR$ and $CP'R$ are congruent, while triangles $CRH$ and $CRP'$ are mirror images with respect to $AC$. Therefore there is a rotation sending $\triangle BRP$ to $\triangle CRH$. Hence $\triangle PRH\sim\triangle CBR$, and so

$$
\angle HPR=\angle ACB.
$$
