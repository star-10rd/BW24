Solution:

Note that $\angle ADC + \angle CDP + \angle BCD + \angle DCP = 360^{\circ}$ (see Figure 1). Thus $\angle ADP = 360^{\circ} - \angle BCD - \angle DCP = \angle BCP$. As we have $|DP| = |CP|$ and $|AD| = |BC|$, the triangles $ADP$ and $BCP$ are congruent and $|AP| = |BP|$. Moreover, $\angle APB = 60^{\circ}$ since $\angle DPC = 60^{\circ}$ and $\angle DPA = \angle CPB$.
