Let $T = PC \cap AB$. Then $\angle BTC = 90^\circ - \angle PCB = 90^\circ - \angle PDB = 90^\circ - \angle SBD = \angle BSC$, thus points $B$, $S$, $T$, $C$ are concyclic. So $\angle TSC = 90^\circ$, and therefore $TS \parallel BD$. Hence
$$
[DSR] = [DTR] = [DTB] - [TBR] = [CTB] - [TBR] = [CRB] = [ARB],
$$
where $[F]$ denotes the area of $F$.
