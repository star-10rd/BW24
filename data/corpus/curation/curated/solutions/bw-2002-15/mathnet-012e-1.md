Solution:

Suppose that the side of the cube is $1$ and the spider sits at the middle of one of the edges. Then the shortest path to the middle of the opposite edge has length $2$. However, if the fly goes to a point on this edge at distance $s$ from the middle, then the length of the shortest path is
$$
\min \left(\sqrt{4+s^{2}}, \sqrt{\frac{9}{4}+\left(\frac{3}{2}-s\right)^{2}}\right) .
$$
If $0 < s < (3-\sqrt{7}) / 2$ then this expression is greater than $2$.
