Without loss of generality it may be assumed that $a \le b \le c$. Observe that
$$
((a-t)+b)(b+(c+t)) = (a+b)(b+c) - t(c-a) - t^2 < (a+b)(b+c).
$$
for $t > 0$.
The initial numbers $a, b, c$ satisfy the triangle inequality $a + b > c$. Choose $t$ such that
$$
(a-t)+b=c+t,
$$
it is clear that $0 < t < a$. If we replace in the inequality under consideration $a$ and $c$ with $a-t, c+t$, the sums $a+c$ and $a+b+c$ remain unchanged, and the product $(a+b)(b+c)$ decreases. Hence it is sufficient to prove the given inequality for positive $a, b, c$ under assumption $a+b=c$. In this case
$$
4 \cdot \frac{a+b}{a+b+c} \cdot \frac{a+c}{2c} \cdot \frac{b+c}{2c} = 4 \cdot \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{a+c}{2c} \cdot \frac{b+c}{2c} = \frac{ab+c(a+b)+c^2}{2c^2} = \frac{ab+2c^2}{2c^2} > 1.
$$
