Given are three real numbers $a, b, c$ such that $0 < a, b, c < \frac{a+b+c}{2}$ (the inequality $a, b, c < \frac{a+b+c}{2}$ is equivalent to the triangle inequality). Introduce the variables
$$
x = \frac{-a + b + c}{2(a + b + c)}, \quad y = \frac{a - b + c}{2(a + b + c)} \quad \text{and} \quad z = \frac{a + b - c}{2(a + b + c)}
$$
The conditions imposed on $a, b$ and $c$ are equivalent to
$$
0 < x, y, z, \quad \text{and} \quad x + y + z = \frac{1}{2}
$$
Clearly
$$
\frac{b+c}{a+b+c} = \frac{1}{2} + x, \quad \frac{c+a}{a+b+c} = \frac{1}{2} + y \quad \text{and} \quad \frac{a+b}{a+b+c} = \frac{1}{2} + z
$$
Therefore we have:
$$
\begin{align*} \frac{(a+b)(a+c)(b+c)}{(a+b+c)^3} &= \frac{b+c}{a+b+c} \cdot \frac{c+a}{a+b+c} \cdot \frac{a+b}{a+b+c} \\ &= \left(\frac{1}{2} + x\right) \cdot \left(\frac{1}{2} + y\right) \cdot \left(\frac{1}{2} + z\right) \\ &= \frac{1}{8} + \frac{x+y+z}{4} + \frac{yz+zx+xy}{2} + xyz \\ &= \frac{1}{8} + \frac{1/2}{4} + \frac{yz+zx+xy}{2} + xyz \\ &> \frac{1}{4} \end{align*}
$$
