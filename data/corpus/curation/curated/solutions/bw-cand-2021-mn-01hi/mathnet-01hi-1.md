Let $\gamma$ be the angle between the sides $a$ and $b$. We have
$$
\begin{aligned}
a^2 + b^2 &\ge 2ab \\
&\ge 2ab \sin(\gamma - 30^\circ) \\
&= 2ab [\sin(\gamma) \cos(30^\circ) - \cos(\gamma) \sin(30^\circ)] \\
&= ab \sin(\gamma)\sqrt{3} - ab \cos(\gamma) \\
&= 2\sqrt{3}A - \frac{1}{2}(a^2 + b^2 - c^2),
\end{aligned}
$$
which implies the desired inequality.
