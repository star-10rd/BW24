Solution:
The line $y=\frac{p}{q} x$ contains the diagonal of the rectangle with vertices $(0,0)$, $(q, 0)$, $(q, p)$ and $(0, p)$ and passes through no points with integer coordinates in the interior of that rectangle. For $k=1,2, \ldots, q-1$ the summand $\left\lfloor\frac{k p}{q}\right\rfloor$ counts the number of interior points of the rectangle lying below the diagonal $y=\frac{p}{q} x$ and having $x$-coordinate equal to $k$. Therefore the sum in consideration counts all interior points with integer coordinates below the diagonal, which is exactly half the number of all points with integer coordinates in the interior of the rectangle, i.e. $\frac{1}{2} \cdot(p-1)(q-1)$.
