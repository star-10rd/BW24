Let $\Gamma$ have centre $O$ and radius $r$. Let $O'$ be the third vertex of the equilateral triangle $AOO'$ (clockwise). Then the locus of $C$ is a circle with centre $O'$ and radius $r$: for consider the triangles $AOB$ and $AO'C$. Then $AO = AO'$ and $BA = CA$ (by construction: equilateral triangles) and $\angle BAO = 60^\circ - \angle O'AB = \angle CAO'$. So the two triangles are congruent (two sides and an included angle) and hence $BO = CO'$. Thus $C$ lies on a circle with centre $O'$ and radius $r$.
