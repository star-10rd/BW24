Let $b$, $r$ and $g$ be the number of blue, red and green balls, respectively. From the probabilities stated we get $\frac{2bg}{100 \cdot 99} = \frac{8}{100}$ and $\frac{2(rg + rb + gb)}{99 \cdot 100} = \frac{58}{100}$, and hence
$$
2bg = 8 \cdot 99 \quad \text{and} \quad 2(rg + rb + gb) = 58 \cdot 99.
$$
From the first equation we see $11$ divides exactly one of $b$ and $g$, and hence from the second $11$ must also divide $r$. Assume w.l.o.g. that $11$ divides $g$ and let $g = 11g'$. Since $r + b + g = 100$ we have $b \equiv 1 \pmod{11}$. The first equation is now $bg' = 36$, and hence $b = 1$ or $b = 12$. If $b = 12$ we get $g = 11 \cdot 36 > 100$ which is impossible. The only solution is therefore $b = 12$, $g = 33$ (the other way around) and $r = 55$.
