Solution:

Squaring both sides of the given inequalities we get
$$
\left\{
\begin{array}{l}
a^{2} \geq (b+c)^{2} \\
b^{2} \geq (c+a)^{2} \\
c^{2} \geq (a+b)^{2}
\end{array}
\right.
$$
Adding these three inequalities and rearranging, we get $(a+b+c)^{2} \leq 0$. Clearly equality must hold, and we have $a+b+c=0$.
