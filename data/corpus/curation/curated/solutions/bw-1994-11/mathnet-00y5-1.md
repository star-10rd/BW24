Solution:

We have $\angle NAS = \angle NBS = 90^{\circ}$ (see Figure 1). Thus, the triangles $NA'S$ and $NSA$ are similar. Also, the triangles $B'NS$ and $SNB$ are similar and the triangles $NSA$ and $SNB$ are congruent. Hence, the triangles $NA'S$ and $B'NS$ are similar which implies $\frac{SA'}{SN} = \frac{SN}{SB'}$ and $SA' \cdot SB' = SN^2$.
