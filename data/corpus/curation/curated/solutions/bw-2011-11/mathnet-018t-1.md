Let $O$ be the centre of $C$. Then $P$, $R$, $S$, and $O$ are points on a circle $C'$ with diameter $OP$, equal to the radius of $C$. The segment $RS$ is a chord in this circle subtending the angle $AOC$ or its supplementary angle. Since the angle as well as radius of $C'$ are independent of $P$, so is $RS$.
