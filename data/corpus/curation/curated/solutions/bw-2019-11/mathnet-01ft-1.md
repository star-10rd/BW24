Since $\{ACMP\}$ is cyclic, we have $\angle RPQ = \angle ACM$. Moreover, $\angle PQR = 90^\circ = \angle CMA$. Hence $\triangle PQR \sim \triangle CMA$. It follows that
$$
\frac{PR}{PQ} = \frac{CA}{CM}. \qquad (1)
$$
Similarly, $\angle PMB = \angle PAC$ and $\angle BPM = 90^\circ = \angle CPA$. Hence $\triangle BPM \sim \triangle CPA$. It follows that
$$
\frac{CA}{BM} = \frac{CP}{BP}. \qquad (2)
$$
Using (1), (2), and the equality $BM = CM$ we obtain
$$
\frac{PR}{PQ} = \frac{CA}{CM} = \frac{CA}{BM} = \frac{CP}{BP}. \qquad (3)
$$
Using (3) and $\angle QPB = 90^\circ = \angle RPC$ we obtain $\triangle QPB \sim \triangle RPC$. In particular, $\angle RCP = \angle PBQ$. Hence
$$
\angle RCP = \angle PBQ = \angle CBA - \angle MBP = \angle ACB - \angle ACP = \angle PCB.
$$
