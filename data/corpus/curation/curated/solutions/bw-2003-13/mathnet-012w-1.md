Solution:

From rectangular triangle $BAP$ we have $BP \cdot BF = AB^{2} = BE^{2}$. Therefore the circumference through $F$ and $P$ touching the line $BC$ between $B$ and $C$ touches it at $E$.

Analogously, the circumference through $P$ and $G$ touching the line $BC$ between $B$ and $C$ touches it at $E$. But there is only one circumference touching $BC$ at $E$ and passing through $P$.
