Again we call $x_{2011}$ also $x_0$. Taking the square on both sides of all the equations and adding the results, we get
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{2011} (x_{n-1} + x_n)^2 = 4 \sum_{n=1}^{2011} x_n'^2 = 4 \sum_{n=1}^{2011} x_n^2,
$$
which can be transformed with some algebra into
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{2011} (x_{n-1} - x_n)^2 = 0.
$$
Hence the assertion follows.
