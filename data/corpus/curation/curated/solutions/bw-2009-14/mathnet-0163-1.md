We can construct such triangles $A_i$ with angles $\alpha$, $i\alpha$, $(2n - i)\alpha$, where $\alpha = \frac{\pi}{2n+1}$. These triangles are non-similar to each other as the biggest angle of each triangle is different. Triangles $A_i$ and $A_{i+1}$ can be joined into a bigger triangle, as the largest angle of $A_i$ and second largest angle of $A_{i+1}$ add up to $\pi$.
