Solution:

Draw the parallelogram $A B C A'$, with $A A' \parallel B C$. Then $M$ lies on $B A'$, and $B M = \frac{1}{3} B A'$. So $M$ is on the homothetic image (centre $B$, dilation $1 / 3$) of the circle with centre $C$ and radius $A B$, which meets $B C$ at $D$ and $E$. The image meets $B C$ at $P$ and $Q$. So $\angle P M Q = 90^{\circ}$.
