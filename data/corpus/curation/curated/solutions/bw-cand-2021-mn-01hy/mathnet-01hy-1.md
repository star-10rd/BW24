Refer to figure 22. Let us start with proving a known simple lemma stating that $\angle BYC = 90^\circ$. To that end, as $\angle IEC = 90^\circ$, it is enough to prove that $IEYC$ is cyclic. Indeed:
$$
\begin{align*} 
\angle CEY &= \angle AEF \\ 
&= 90^\circ - \frac{1}{2} \angle BAC \\ 
&= 90^\circ - \frac{1}{2} (180^\circ - \angle ABC - \angle ACB) \\ 
&= \frac{1}{2} \angle ABC + \frac{1}{2} \angle ACB \\ 
&= \angle IBC + \angle ICB \\ 
&= \angle YIC. 
\end{align*}
$$
This shows that $YIEC$ is cyclic. Since $AC$ is tangent to the incircle at $E$,
$$
\angle BIC = \angle CYI = \angle CEI = 90^\circ
$$
Similarly, we may prove that $\angle BZC = 90^\circ$.
All this implies that $BYZC$ is cyclic with $M$ being its centre. Hence $N$ is the midpoint of its chord $YZ$ and therefore $MN \perp EF$. However, $AI \perp EF$, so $MN \parallel AI$ as desired. $\square$
