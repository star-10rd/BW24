Denoting the new number
$$
g = \frac{abc}{ab + bc + ca},
$$
its reciprocal is
$$
\frac{1}{g} = \frac{ab + bc + ca}{abc} = \frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b} + \frac{1}{c}.
$$
The sum of all reciprocals is therefore invariantly
$$
\sum_{k=1}^{2m} \frac{1}{k(k+1)} = \sum_{k=1}^{2m} \left( \frac{1}{k} - \frac{1}{k+1} \right) = 1 - \frac{1}{2m+1} = \frac{2m}{2m+1}.
$$
The other remaining number $x$ can then be calculated from the equation
$$
\frac{1}{x} + \frac{3}{4} = \frac{2m}{2m+1},
$$
leading to
$$
x = \frac{4(2m+1)}{2m-3} = 4 + \frac{16}{2m-3} > 4.
$$
