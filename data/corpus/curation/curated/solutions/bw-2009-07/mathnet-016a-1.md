Observe that $p \mid a^4 + b^4 + (-a-b)^4 = 2(a^2 + ab + b^2)^2$. Thus, since $p \ne 2$, we have $p \mid a^2 + ab + b^2$ which implies that $p \mid (a^2 + ab + b^2)(a - b) = a^3 - b^3$. From this fact and Fermat's little theorem we obtain the following congruences, with $p = 6n - 1$:
$$
b \equiv b^p \equiv b^p b^{p-1} = b^{3(4n-1)} \equiv a^{3(4n-1)} = a^p a^{p-1} \equiv a \quad \mod p.
$$
Therefore $a \equiv b \pmod p$ and, similarly, $a \equiv c \pmod p$. Consequently,
$$
0 \equiv a + b + c \equiv 3a \equiv 3b \equiv 3c \quad \mod p
$$
so $p \mid a$, $b$, $c$, in view of $p \ne 3$.
