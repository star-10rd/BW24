Since
$$
(n + 2)^2 - (n + 1)^2 - n^2 + (n - 1)^2 = 4,
$$
we can always choose 8 consecutive indices such that the corresponding terms in the sum add up to zero. As the first term is a zero anyway, it suffices to note that $1^2 = 1$, $2^2 - 1^2 = 3$, $3^2 - 2^2 - 1^2 = 4$, $4^2 - 3^2 - 2^2 - 1^2 = 2$, $5^2 - 4^2 - 3^2 + 2^2 - 1^2 = 3$ and $6^2 - 5^2 - 4^2 + 3^2 - 2^2 + 1^2 = 1$.
