Let $a = x - y$ and $b = y - z$, then
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{1}{(x-y)^2} + \frac{1}{(y-z)^2} + \frac{1}{(z-x)^2} &= \frac{1}{a^2} + \frac{1}{b^2} + \frac{1}{(a+b)^2} \\
&= \frac{b^2(a+b)^2 + a^2(a+b)^2 + a^2b^2}{a^2b^2(a+b)^2} \\
&= \left( \frac{a^2 + b^2 + ab}{ab(a+b)} \right)^2.
\end{aligned}
$$
On the other hand, $2014$ is not a square of a rational number. Hence such numbers do not exist.
