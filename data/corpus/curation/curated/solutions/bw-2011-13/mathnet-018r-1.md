We consider oriented angles modulo $180^\circ$. From the cyclic quadrilaterals $APES$, $BQEP$, $PQRS$, $CREQ$, $DSER$ and $\triangle DCE \sim \triangle ABF$ we get
$$
\begin{align*}
\angle AEB &= \angle EAB + \angle ABE = \angle ESP + \angle PQE \\
&= \angle ESR + \angle RSP + \angle PQR + \angle RQE \\
&= \angle ESR + \angle RQE = \angle EDC + \angle DCE \\
&= \angle DEC = \angle AFB,
\end{align*}
$$
so the quadrilateral $AEBF$ is cyclic. By Ptolemy we then have
$$
EF \cdot AB = AE \cdot BF + BE \cdot AF.
$$
This transforms by $AB : BF : AF = DC : CE : DE$ into
$$
EF \cdot CD = AE \cdot CE + BE \cdot DE.
$$

Since the expression on the right of this equation is invariant under cyclic permutation of the vertices of the quadrilateral $ABCD$, the asserted equation follows immediately.
