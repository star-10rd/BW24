Let
$$
X = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{F_n}{10^{n+1}}
$$
Then
$$
X = \frac{1}{10^2} + \frac{1}{10^3} + \frac{2}{10^4} + \frac{3}{10^5} + \frac{5}{10^6} + \frac{8}{10^7} + \frac{13}{10^8} + \dots
$$
So
$$
10X = \frac{1}{10} + \frac{1}{10^2} + \frac{2}{10^3} + \frac{3}{10^4} + \frac{5}{10^5} + \frac{8}{10^6} + \frac{13}{10^7} + \dots
$$
and
$$
100X = 1 + \frac{1}{10} + \frac{2}{10^2} + \frac{3}{10^3} + \frac{5}{10^4} + \frac{8}{10^5} + \frac{13}{10^6} + \dots
$$
Then $100X - 10X - X = 1$ (using the basic property of the Fibonacci numbers).
$$
\text{So } X = \frac{1}{89}
$$
