Solution:

Let $X$ be the point symmetric to $B$ with respect to $AN$, and let $Y$ be the point symmetric to $C$ with respect to $DN$ (see Figure 3).
Then
$$
\angle XNY = 180^\circ - 2 \cdot (180^\circ - 135^\circ) = 90^\circ
$$
and $|NX| = |NY| = \frac{|BC|}{2}$. Therefore, $|XY| = \frac{|BC|}{\sqrt{2}}$.
Moreover, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
& |AX| = |AB| \text{ and } |DY| = |DC|. \text{ Consequently, } \\
& \qquad |AD| \leqslant |AX| + |XY| + |YD| = |AB| + \frac{|BC|}{\sqrt{2}} + |DC|.
\end{aligned}
$$
