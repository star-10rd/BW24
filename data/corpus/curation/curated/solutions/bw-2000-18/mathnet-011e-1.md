Solution:
$x = y = 1 + \sqrt{2}$.

Note that
$$
x + \frac{1}{x} + 2 - 2 \sqrt{2x+1} = \frac{x^2 + 2x + 1 - 2x \sqrt{2x+1}}{x} = \frac{1}{x}(x - \sqrt{2x+1})^2.
$$
Hence the original equation can be rewritten as
$$
\frac{1}{x}(x - \sqrt{2x+1})^2 + \frac{1}{y}(y - \sqrt{2y+1})^2 = 0.
$$
For $x, y > 0$ this gives $x - \sqrt{2x+1} = 0$ and $y - \sqrt{2y+1} = 0$. It follows that the only solution is $x = y = 1 + \sqrt{2}$.
