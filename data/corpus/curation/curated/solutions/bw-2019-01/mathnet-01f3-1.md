From AM-GM inequality we have $\sqrt{xyz} \le \frac{xy+z}{2}$. Hence it suffices to prove that
$$
\frac{x^3 - y^3 + z^3 + 1}{3} \ge (x - y)(xy + z),
$$
which is equivalent to
$$
\frac{(x - y)^3 + z^3 + 1}{3} \ge z(x - y).
$$
This inequality follows directly from AM-GM inequality applied to the numbers $(x-y)^3$, $z^3$ and $1$.
