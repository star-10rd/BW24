We will show that $719$ is a prime factor of given number. All congruences are considered modulo $719$. By Wilson's theorem $718! \equiv -1$. What is more $713 \cdot 714 \cdot 715 \cdot 716 \cdot 717 \cdot 718 \equiv (-6)(-5)(-4)(-3)(-2)(-1) \equiv 720 \equiv 1$. Hence $712! \equiv -1$ so $712! + 1$ is divisible by $719$. Note that $719$ is the smallest prime greater than $712$.
