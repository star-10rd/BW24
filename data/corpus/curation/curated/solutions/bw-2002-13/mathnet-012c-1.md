Solution:

Let $E$ be the other point on the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$ such that $|AB| = |EB|$. Let $D'$ be the point of intersection of side $AC$ and the line perpendicular to side $BC$, passing through $E$. Then $\angle ECB = \angle BCA$ and the triangle $ECD'$ is isosceles. As $ED' \perp BC$, the triangle $BED'$ is also isosceles and $|BE| = |BD'|$ implying $D = D'$. Hence, the points $E, D, F$ lie on one line. We now have
$$
\angle EFB + \angle FDA = \angle BCA + \angle EDC = 90^\circ.
$$
The required result now follows.
