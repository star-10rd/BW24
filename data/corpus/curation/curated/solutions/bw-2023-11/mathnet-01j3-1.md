Let $JK$ intersect $BC$ at $X$. We'll prove a key claim:

**Claim:** $\triangle BEK$ is similar to $\triangle BAX$.

*Proof.* Note that $\angle EAB = 90^\circ = \angle KXB$. Also, since $BJ$ bisects $\angle CBA$, we get $\angle ABE = \angle JBX = \angle XBK$. Hence $\triangle EBA \sim \triangle KBX$. From that, we see that the spiral similarity that sends the line segment $EA$ to $KX$ has center $B$. So the spiral similarity that sends the line segment $EK$ to $AX$ has center $B$. Thus $\triangle BEK \sim BAX$. $\square$

In a similar manner, we get $\triangle CFK$ is similar to $\triangle CAX$.

\angle FKE + \angle FJE &= \angle FKE + \angle BKC \\

&= 360^\circ - \angle AXB - \angle CXA \\ 
&= 360^\circ - 180^\circ \\ 
&= 180^\circ 
\end{align*}
$$
as desired.
