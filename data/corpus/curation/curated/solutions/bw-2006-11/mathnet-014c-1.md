Solution:

Denote the sides of the triangle by $a$, $b$ and $c$ and its altitudes by $h_{a}$, $h_{b}$ and $h_{c}$. Then we know that $h_{a}=12$, $h_{b}=15$ and $h_{c}=20$. By the well known relation $a : b = h_{b} : h_{a}$ it follows $b = \frac{h_{a}}{h_{b}} a = \frac{12}{15} a = \frac{4}{5} a$. Analogously, $c = \frac{h_{a}}{h_{c}} a = \frac{12}{20} a = \frac{3}{5} a$.

Thus half of the triangle's circumference is $s = \frac{1}{2}(a + b + c) = \frac{1}{2}\left(a + \frac{4}{5} a + \frac{3}{5} a\right) = \frac{6}{5} a$.

For the area $\Delta$ of the triangle we have $\Delta = \frac{1}{2} a h_{a} = \frac{1}{2} a \cdot 12 = 6a$, and also by the well known Heron formula $\Delta = \sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)} = \sqrt{\frac{6}{5} a \cdot \frac{1}{5} a \cdot \frac{2}{5} a \cdot \frac{3}{5} a} = \sqrt{\frac{6^{2}}{5^{4}} a^{4}} = \frac{6}{25} a^{2}$.

Hence, $6a = \frac{6}{25} a^{2}$, and we get $a = 25$ ($b = 20$, $c = 15$) and consequently $\Delta = 150$.
