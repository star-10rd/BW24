# P3 research zone

Files here are evidence/checkpoints, not canonical corpus truth. They may be incomplete or provisional.

Accepted facts are promoted deliberately into `../identity/` or `../curation/`. P3A validation checks that the research checkpoint files are valid JSON objects, while later phases may add stronger task-specific schemas.

`reviews.jsonl` is the typed queue for unresolved questions that directly concern canonical appearances, versions, source matches, content, or assets.
